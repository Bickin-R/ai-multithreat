No source annotation, image, manifest, or split assignment was changed.

MANIFEST / REVIEW STATUS

- val.jsonl: 20 images, 95 person boxes
- test.jsonl: 19 images, 85 person boxes
- All 103 rows omit the `reviewed` field. Therefore none can be certified as

SESSION / SCENE LEAKAGE ASSESSMENT
- Validation images are named session_01_000001.jpg through
  session_01_000020.jpg. Test images continue as session_01_000021.jpg through
  session_01_000039.jpg. This is a sequential filename series split at the
  validation/test boundary, with no source_group or timestamp evidence to show
  these are independent captures.
- Exploratory 64-bit grayscale difference-hash comparison found 11 of 19 test
  images with a nearest validation image at distance <=6 bits. Examples:
  test 000021 vs val 000010 (3 bits), test 000022 vs val 000010 (4),
  test 000026 vs val 000011 (4), test 000035 vs val 000011 (3), and
  test 000036 vs val 000010 (4). This is a scene/frame similarity signal, not
  proof of identical frames; exact image hashes found no duplicates.
- Train filenames use person_session_01; validation/test use session_01.
  Train and validation/test views show a visually similar room and camera
  placement. The distinct naming and capture metadata suggest separate named
  sources, but do not establish distinct physical sessions, people, or scenes.
- Conclusion: validation/test independence is not established and adjacent
  frames from one scene are a credible leakage risk. Confirm the collection
  history with a human before using these splits for model selection claims.
  Do not use the test split to tune or select future checkpoints.
